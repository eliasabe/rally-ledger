How points work: everyone starts at 0. Each player carries a hidden Elo-style rating (baseline 1000, K=32) that updates after every match based on how likely they were expected to win. The 0–100 number shown everywhere is that rating compressed onto a scale where 1000 reads as 0 and gains taper as they approach 100 — so beating higher-rated opponents earns more, beating lower-rated opponents earns less, and a bad run can't get permanently stuck at the floor. A tied match leaves ratings untouched.

Confirmations: a newly logged match sits as pending and has no effect on anyone's rating until the player who did not log it confirms it — never the person who recorded the result. Disputing a match rejects it permanently; it stays visible in history, struck through, but never counts.

Joining: anyone with the link can request to join, but a new player only appears on the ladder once the admin approves the request — enforced by the page itself, not just good manners.
